How it works

Three steps. Zero friction.

No new tabs, no copy-paste. Explainly lives right inside the page you're reading.

01

Highlight any text

Select a word, sentence, or whole paragraph on any site — articles, docs, PDFs, research papers.

02

Pick a depth

Tap ELI5, Normal, Deep, or Why? — switch modes anytime to go shallower or deeper.

03

Read it stream in

A clear explanation appears in a popup, streaming word-by-word. Save it, hear it, or move on.
